This benchmark answers the following questions:

- Are SCPs and RCPs enabled on the organization root?
- Which SCPs, RCPs and declarative policies are not attached to any root, OU or account?
- Which policies are close to the AWS Organizations size limit?
- Which roots, OUs and accounts are close to the SCP or RCP attachment limit?
- Which SCP allow lists are overridden by FullAWSAccess on the same target?
- Which SCP and RCP statements are duplicated across policies?
- Which attached SCP deny statements have no break-glass exemption?
